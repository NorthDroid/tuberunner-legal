Tube Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Tube Runner is a puzzle game made by northdroid. This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: we do not collect personal data, and you do not need an account to play.

Information we collect

None. Tube Runner does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, such as the levels you have completed, your coins and your settings, is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Technical information sent by the game engine

Tube Runner is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may automatically send anonymous technical information, such as the device model, operating system version and graphics hardware, to Unity Technologies to help improve the engine. This information does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for details.

Advertising and purchases

The current version contains no third-party advertising, no analytics services and no real-money purchases.

Beta testing

If you test Tube Runner through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share information with us such as crash reports, device and usage data, and any feedback you choose to send. This is handled under Apple's privacy policy and the TestFlight terms.

Children

Tube Runner does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the game starts collecting any data, for example through ads or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: appdeveloper@northdroid.com
