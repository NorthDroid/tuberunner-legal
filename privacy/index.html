Tube Runner 3D Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Tube Runner 3D is a puzzle game made by northdroid. This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: we do not collect personal data ourselves and you do not need an account to play, but the game shows optional video ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show them.

Information we collect

None. Tube Runner 3D does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we have no servers that receive data about you.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, such as the levels you have completed, your coins and your settings, is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

You can choose to watch a video ad to get a helper or bonus coins. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when the game asks. You can change this at any time in the iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

You can buy coins with real money. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only that a purchase went through, so the coins can be added.

Technical information sent by the game engine

Tube Runner 3D is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may automatically send anonymous technical information, such as the device model, operating system version and graphics hardware, to Unity Technologies to help improve the engine. This information does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for details.

Beta testing

If you test Tube Runner 3D through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share information with us such as crash reports, device and usage data, and any feedback you choose to send. This is handled under Apple's privacy policy and the TestFlight terms.

Children

Tube Runner 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If the data the game handles changes, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.

Contact
